Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your financial information is personal. This Privacy Policy explains what data Spendly collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form, never as plain text).
  • Expense records: the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Basic technical data: standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and request times, used for security and troubleshooting.

Spendly does not connect to your bank accounts or cards, and we never ask for card numbers or banking credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and spending breakdowns.
  • Maintain, protect, and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database on our servers and is kept only for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorised access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

You can request deletion of your account at any time, after which your account details and expense records will be permanently removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads web fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests these fonts from Google, which may receive your IP address and browser information. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

We do not share your account details or expense records with any third party unless required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to access or delete your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
